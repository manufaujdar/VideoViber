# Vision

## What is VideoViber?

VideoViber is an **agentic spec-driven video workspace** designed to bridge the gap between a vague creative idea and an editable first-cut video.

It is **not** a one-shot video generator. It is **not** a thin wrapper around a single AI provider.

VideoViber is a **structured creative workflow** that:

1. Accepts a high-level creative brief (a "vibe")
2. Decomposes it into structured specs (narrative arc, scene breakdown, shot list)
3. Generates shots from multiple AI video providers
4. Enables non-destructive editing (extend, regenerate, swap, trim, reorder)
5. Assembles an editable first cut for export

## Why Now?

Current AI video generation models produce **short clips** (4–10 seconds). No single model can produce a full narrative video. The real product opportunity is in:

- **Structured decomposition**: Turning a vague idea into actionable shot specs
- **Multi-provider orchestration**: Using the best model for each shot
- **Iterative refinement**: Extend, regenerate, and compare variants
- **Timeline assembly**: Stitching clips into a coherent sequence
- **Non-destructive editing**: Every change is versioned, every edit is reversible

## Core Beliefs

1. **Specs, not chat.** Creative intent should be decomposed into structured data — not a chat conversation.
2. **Provider-agnostic.** The best provider for a shot changes over time. Abstract from day one.
3. **Async-native.** Video generation takes minutes. The UX must handle this gracefully.
4. **BYOK-first.** Users bring their own API keys. No managed billing until proven demand.
5. **Editor-first.** The workspace is a creative tool, not a demo.

## Target User

Creative professionals, indie filmmakers, marketing teams, and content creators who want to go from idea to rough cut faster — without manually prompting and stitching individual clips.

## Success Metric

**Time from vague idea to editable first cut: under 10 minutes.**

This is the singular metric that defines whether VideoViber delivers value.
